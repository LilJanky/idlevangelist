# IdleVangelist --- Generation 1 Product Specification

## Product Vision

IdleVangelist is a mobile-first Christian incremental game combining the
immediate accessibility and automation satisfaction of idle business
games with the active engagement, escalating numbers, build choices,
bosses, and prestige satisfaction of clicker/active incremental games.

The first playable release is a **25--35 minute Generation 1 vertical
slice**. It should feel like a small complete game, not a tech demo.

## Player Fantasy

The player is a fictional male street evangelist in a bleak, near-future
city. He begins alone, faithfully proclaiming the gospel in a moving
public crowd. Over time, ministry expands through literature, Bibles,
helpers, Bible study, discipleship, and other believers.

The player is responsible for faithful action. The player is never
presented as the sovereign cause of regeneration.

## Experience Pillars

### 1. Immediately Understandable

Within seconds, the player can engage a highlighted passerby and see
useful feedback. The first meaningful purchase should occur roughly
within the first minute.

### 2. Idle-Friendly

The ministry gradually works without constant tapping. Closing the game
does not make the player feel punished.

### 3. Active Play Matters

Watching the crowd, catching special encounters, using Scripture
abilities, maintaining encounter chains, and choosing when to challenge
the boss materially accelerate progress.

### 4. Meaningful Builds

Upgrades gradually support active witness, Scripture/encounter play, or
ministry automation. Do not ask the player to select a class at the
start.

### 5. Christian Without Requiring Christian Participation

The game is openly and unmistakably Christian. A player may skip/ignore
most dialogue and still understand the mechanics. Christianity is not
hidden behind generic spirituality.

### 6. Generational Progress

Prestige is called **Next Generation**. The city and ministry retain
visible consequences of previous generations. Prestige represents
multiplication and legacy, not history being erased.

## Audience

Primary: - Mobile/casual incremental players - Players who enjoy active
optimization and number growth - Christian players who recognize the
theological and apologetic material

Secondary: - Non-Christian players who enjoy the game loop and may
engage with the content voluntarily

Do not require quizzes, doctrinal tests, or forced reading to progress.

## Generation 1 Scope

Required: - One city-street environment - One protagonist - Moving
background crowd - Standard encounters - Special timed crowd
encounters - Seeds economy - Funds as a secondary mundane-resource
economy - Five passive ministry lines - Disciples/automation - Scripture
abilities - Approximately 20 upgrades - One major boss encounter with
phases - Rare conversion events governed by hidden outcome logic -
Offline progression - Save/load - First prestige: Next Generation -
Faithfulness prestige currency - Small first prestige tree - Visual
world-state change after prestige - Debug tools - Automated tests -
Sound/music controls

Not required: - Generation 2 gameplay - Multiple cities - Character
creator - Inventory/equipment loot system - Multiplayer - Accounts/cloud
saves - Backend/server - Procedural open world - Complex branching
narrative - Dozens of bosses - Full historical age system - Achievements
platform integration - Monetization, ads, microtransactions, or premium
currency

## Product Tone

Serious about Christianity; approachable about itself.

Humor is welcome in characterization and ordinary human moments. Do not
make unbelievers uniformly stupid, evil, or grotesque. Objections should
be recognizable arguments a real person might make.

The near-future city may be oppressive, commercialized, lonely, and
spiritually apathetic, but it should not become a grimdark caricature.

## Visual Direction

-   16-bit-inspired pixel-art presentation
-   Mobile portrait first
-   Logical target approximately 390 × 844
-   Muted/darker Generation 1 city
-   Background pedestrians lower-detail and lower-contrast
-   Focused encounter characters become clearer and more visually
    prominent
-   Kingdom/generational progress is reflected in environmental
    restoration, warmth, community, and visible ministry
-   Avoid photorealism and asset-heavy animation
-   Original visual identity; do not imitate copyrighted characters or
    exact art styles

## Success Criteria

A successful first build causes a player to: 1. Understand the core loop
without reading a manual. 2. Make a first purchase quickly. 3. Notice
that active attention improves progress. 4. Experience automation
becoming satisfying. 5. Discover that Scripture has gameplay relevance.
6. Care when a conversion event occurs. 7. Anticipate and defeat/retry
the boss. 8. Reach Next Generation around the 30-minute mark. 9.
Immediately understand why a second generation would be faster and
different.
