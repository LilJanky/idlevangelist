# IdleVangelist --- Theology and Content Guardrails

This file has the highest authority over game content and mechanics.

## Core Rule

**The player is responsible for faithfulness; God is sovereign over
regeneration and spiritual growth.**

The game may reward faithful proclamation, Scripture use, preparation,
prayer, perseverance, discipleship, stewardship, and multiplication. It
must never teach or imply that a human mechanically produces
regeneration.

> "I planted, Apollos watered, but God gave the growth." --- 1
> Corinthians 3:6\
> "So neither he who plants nor he who waters is anything, but only God
> who gives the growth." --- 1 Corinthians 3:7

## Scripture

-   Scripture is authoritative truth, not magic.
-   Scripture abilities are a gameplay abstraction for the power and
    usefulness of God's Word, not spellcasting.
-   Do not depict Bible verses as incantations that force conversion.
-   Contextual Scripture choices may be more useful against particular
    objections, but a verse does not become false because it is not the
    optimal gameplay response.
-   Use Scripture to accompany important victories, generational
    transitions, boss phases, and meaningful encounters.
-   Avoid flooding every screen with text. Players may ignore optional
    content without blocking progression.

## Gospel

When the game states the gospel, it must center on: - God's holiness and
authority - Human sin and guilt - Jesus Christ as truly God and truly
man - Christ's substitutionary death and bodily resurrection -
Repentance and faith in Christ - Salvation as grace, not earned merit

Avoid vague messages such as "believe in yourself," generic
spirituality, or salvation through moral improvement.

## Conversion

Conversion events may occur after faithful encounters, but: - They are
not guaranteed by clicking speed, damage, money, equipment, charisma, or
a deterministic sequence. - They use hidden outcome logic that the
player cannot reduce to "perform X and force conversion." - Player
upgrades may create more opportunities, clearer proclamation,
persistence, or more encounters, but never purchase regeneration. -
Conversion should feel significant and relatively uncommon in
Generation 1. - Do not award a currency named Souls. - Do not frame
human beings as enemies to kill. - A person may be successfully answered
and remain unconvinced. - A person may respond unexpectedly after modest
visible effort. - Some people may never visibly respond during the
generation.

John 3:8 and 1 Corinthians 3:6--7 are useful governing texts.

## Encounters and "Combat"

The game may use: - Resistance meters - Truth/proclamation numbers -
Critical hits as "Critical Proclamation" or equivalent - Multipliers -
Combos - Boss phases - Cooldowns - Timed opportunities

These represent overcoming an objection, sustaining a conversation,
gaining a hearing, answering an argument, or faithfully completing an
opportunity. They never represent physical or spiritual violence against
the person.

Avoid language such as: - Kill - Damage the atheist - Destroy the
person - Souls harvested - Capture converts

Prefer: - Engage - Proclaim - Answer - Resistance - Opportunity - Seeds
planted - Faithful witness - Conversation completed

## Apologetics

Surface presentation is broadly orthodox Christianity, but apologetic
reasoning should be Scripture-driven and presuppositional in
orientation.

The game should not present human autonomous reason as a neutral court
above God. It may expose worldview inconsistency, appeal to creation and
conscience, challenge neutrality, and proclaim Scripture as
authoritative.

Useful passages include: - Romans 1:18--23 - Proverbs 1:7 - Proverbs
30:5--6 - Colossians 2:8 - 1 Peter 3:15 - 2 Corinthians 10:5

Be charitable. Represent objections recognizably rather than creating
straw men.

## Postmillennial Progression

The game does not need to label itself "postmillennial," but its
generational structure should reflect confidence in Christ's kingdom and
Great Commission.

Key texts: - Matthew 28:18--20 - Psalm 110:1 - Psalm 145:4 - Isaiah
2:2--4 - Habakkuk 2:14

Generation 1 may begin in a dark local situation. Future generational
progression should not require the world to become increasingly hopeless
merely to create stakes.

Next Generation should visually communicate that faithful labor has
historical consequences. The world is not simply erased and reset.

## Broad Orthodoxy

Do not make denominational distinctives required tutorial material in
Generation 1. Avoid unnecessary fights over secondary doctrines.

At the same time: - Do not contradict the authority/sufficiency of
Scripture. - Do not contradict God's sovereignty in salvation. - Do not
portray the church or discipleship as optional to mature Christian
life. - Do not use prosperity-gospel logic. - Do not make money a
measure of divine favor. - Do not use manipulative conversion techniques
as positive mechanics.

## Funds

Money/Funds are mundane stewardship resources only. They may purchase
Bibles, literature, transportation, sound equipment, meeting space,
supplies, and similar material resources.

Money must not: - Purchase conversion probability directly - Purchase
Faithfulness - Function as spiritual power - Be represented as buying
God's favor

## Faithfulness

Faithfulness is the prestige currency and represents enduring
generational fruit/reward as a game abstraction.

Award it primarily for completing faithful ministry milestones and the
generation, not simply for raw conversion count.

Conversions may contribute modestly to end-of-generation statistics or
bonuses, but the optimal Faithfulness strategy must never be "force the
maximum number of conversions."

## Content Safety and Respect

The city may include difficult circumstances and unbelief. Do not use
demeaning stereotypes based on race, disability, poverty, addiction, or
other human conditions. Individuals should be treated as image-bearers
even when their arguments are opposed.
